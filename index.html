Domain Ownership Notice

GovSwift.com is now owned and operated by Swift Passport & Visa Services.

Swift Passport & Visa Services has been serving customers with passport and visa services for over 15 years. GovSwift.com was previously registered and operated by an unrelated third party — not Swift Passport & Visa Services — and its name led many visitors to believe they were dealing with our company. As a result, our team received a large number of calls and complaints from people who had problems with that site, through no fault of our own.

To put a stop to this, Swift Passport & Visa Services retained legal counsel and pursued a formal legal complaint under the Uniform Domain Name Dispute Resolution Policy (UDRP). After review, an independent arbitration panel ruled in our favor, and ownership of the GovSwift.com domain was legally and permanently transferred to Swift Passport & Visa Services.

We want the public to know clearly: Swift Passport & Visa Services had no connection to the previous GovSwift.com website or any issues it may have caused. We're sorry for the frustration this may have caused, but that site was never ours.

If you're looking for a trusted, experienced passport and visa service, we invite you to visit us directly.
